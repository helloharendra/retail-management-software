## What Is ERP Software?

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) software is an integrated business management system that connects multiple departments and business functions through a centralized platform.

Instead of maintaining separate applications and disconnected records, an ERP system helps organizations manage their core operations using shared data, connected workflows, and centralized reporting.

A customized ERP solution can help businesses:

- Integrate multiple departments.
- Centralize organizational data.
- Automate repetitive business processes.
- Manage inventory, purchases, and sales.
- Track employee and operational activities.
- Improve financial and business reporting.
- Make informed decisions using real-time information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between ERP and CRM software?

ERP software integrates multiple business functions such as inventory, purchases, finance, sales, and operations. CRM primarily focuses on customer relationships, leads, sales pipelines, and customer interactions. CRM can also be included as a module within a broader ERP solution.

### Can you develop ERP software from scratch?

Yes. We can analyze your requirements and develop a custom ERP solution with modules designed around your business operations.

### Can ERP software manage multiple branches?

Yes. Multi-branch and multi-location management can be included, with centralized reporting and location-based access depending on the project scope.

### Can I add new modules in the future?

Yes. The system can be planned with a modular architecture so additional functionality can be introduced as business requirements evolve.

### Can ERP software include Android and iOS applications?

Yes. Mobile applications can be developed and integrated with the ERP backend where required.
